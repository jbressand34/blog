Welcome 👋

This blog is a space dedicated to exploring Artificial Intelligence from both a practical and a reflective perspective.

AI is evolving at an extraordinary pace. New models, tools, and paradigms emerge every month. It can be difficult to separate signal from noise, hype from substance, and theory from practical impact.

The goal of this blog is simple:

* To explore AI topics with clarity and intellectual honesty
* To share useful resources and synthesize key ideas
* To connect AI with broader fields that help us understand it better

I am particularly interested in frontier technologies around generative AI (GenAI), but this blog is not limited to that. AI is more than models and benchmarks — it is also about cognition, systems, learning, and problem-solving.

You will find here:

* Technical breakdowns
* Conceptual explorations
* Reflections on emerging trends
* Curated resources worth your time

I do not claim to invent new theories or revolutionary concepts. Instead, I aim to clarify, synthesize, and make complex ideas more accessible.

# About Me

My name is **Jérémy Bressand**.

I have been working in the data field for 7 years and on AI-related projects for the past 2 years. My professional background has led me to approach AI primarily through problem-solving: understanding constraints, designing solutions, and grounding ideas in reality.

At the same time, I am deeply interested in the conceptual foundations of intelligence.

To better understand AI, I draw inspiration from multiple disciplines:

* Neuroscience
* Mathematics
* Psychology
* Philosophy
* Biology

I believe that artificial intelligence cannot be fully understood from a single angle. It requires both rigor and curiosity. Both structure and exploration.

My approach combines:

* A factual, analytical mindset
* An intuitive and exploratory perspective

This blog reflects that dual approach.
